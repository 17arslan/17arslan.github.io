Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026  ·  Applies to all mobile games published by AMG (ArsMobileGames)

AMG (ArsMobileGames), referred to as "we", "us" or "our", develops and publishes free-to-play mobile games. This Privacy Policy explains what information our games may collect, how it is used, and who it may be shared with. It applies to every game we publish, current and future, under the AMG name.

By downloading or using any of our games, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. We do not sell your personal information, and we only share it with the service providers described below, for the purposes described below.

Information We Collect

Most of the data our games need to run — such as saved progress or in-game settings — stays on your device and is never sent to us directly. To keep the game free, improve it over time, and show ads, our games rely on third-party services that may collect some information automatically, described in the next section.

Advertising and Analytics Partners

Our games use Yodo1 Mas, an ad mediation platform, to display advertising. Yodo1 Mas does not serve ads directly; instead it selects among several third-party ad networks for each ad request. Depending on the game and region, this can include networks such as:

  • Google AdMob
  • Meta Audience Network (Facebook)
  • AppLovin
  • Unity Ads
  • ironSource / Unity LevelPlay
  • Liftoff / Vungle
  • Mintegral
  • Pangle (TikTok for Business)

We may add or remove ad networks and analytics tools over time as our games evolve, without needing to change this list for every update. Each of these companies has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data:

  • Google — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Meta / Facebook — facebook.com/policy.php
  • AppLovin — applovin.com/privacy
  • Unity Technologies — unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • ironSource — ironsrc.com privacy policy
  • Vungle / Liftoff — vungle.com/privacy
  • Mintegral — mintegral.com/privacy
  • TikTok / Pangle — tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Yodo1 — yodo1.com/privacy-policy

These partners may collect device identifiers, approximate location, and usage data to serve and measure ads, in line with your device's ad-tracking permissions.

Log Data

If an error occurs while you use one of our games, our third-party tools may automatically collect data sent by your device, such as its IP address, device model, operating system version, app settings, and the date and time of use.

Cookies

Our games do not directly use cookies. However, the third-party tools embedded in our games may use cookies or similar identifiers to collect information and improve their own services. You can usually control this through your device's privacy settings.

Service Providers

We work with third-party companies to run our games, provide the services above, and understand how our games are used. These partners only receive the data needed to perform their specific role and are not permitted to use it for unrelated purposes.

Security

We take reasonable, commercially standard steps to protect the information handled through our games. That said, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Links to Other Sites

Our games may contain links to external sites, such as an ad network's landing page or a social platform. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of any third-party site, and we recommend reviewing their policies separately.

Children's Privacy

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has shared personal information with us, please contact us using the details below.
